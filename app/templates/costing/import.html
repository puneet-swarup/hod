1 · Ingredients

name, unit, cost per unit — one per line. The cost is for one unit, so ₹0.45 for a gram of chocolate, not ₹450 for a kilo. 

Dark chocolate, g, 0.45
Butter, g, 0.55
Sugar, g, 0.045
Eggs, no, 7
Maida, g, 0.04
Nutella, g, 0.38
Walnuts, g, 0.90

2 · Recipes

family, ingredient, quantity, batch yield — the batch yield is optional and defaults to 24. Rows with the same family become one recipe, and every size of that product is costed from it.

The ingredient must already exist — paste the ingredients above first. Quantities are in the ingredient's own unit. 

Nutella Brownie, Dark chocolate, 1200, 24
Nutella Brownie, Butter, 800, 24
Nutella Brownie, Sugar, 600, 24
Nutella Brownie, Eggs, 12, 24
Nutella Brownie, Maida, 300, 24
Nutella Brownie, Nutella, 800, 24
Nutella Brownie, Walnuts, 200, 24

All or nothing: if any line is wrong, nothing is saved. Labour, overhead and wastage are set per recipe on the cost sheet afterwards.
