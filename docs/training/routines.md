# Daily and monthly routines

Two checklists. The daily one takes ten minutes and keeps the kitchen
honest. The monthly one takes half an hour and keeps the money honest.

Nothing here is optional for a bakery that wants reliable reports — most
"the numbers look wrong" problems are a skipped daily routine, not a bug.

---

## Daily — morning (5 minutes)

- [ ] Open **Today**. Read the **Overdue** bucket first. Anything there is
      work you owe someone; decide now whether it ships today.
- [ ] Check **Low stock** at the top. If it is showing, either order the
      ingredient or accept that a product is off the menu today.
- [ ] Look at **Dispatch — Today**: times, delivery vs pickup, and the
      balance column. Any red balance is money to collect at handover.

## Daily — during the day

- [ ] Every order that starts baking: **Mark IN PROGRESS**. This is what
      deducts ingredients — skip it and your stock and COGS are wrong.
- [ ] Every payment, at the moment it arrives: **Record Payment**, with
      the UPI/UTR reference. Do not batch this to the evening.
- [ ] Every delivery: **Mark DELIVERED**. The payment badge is separate
      and does not move by itself.

## Daily — evening (5 minutes)

- [ ] Any order still CONFIRMED whose date was today: move it, or accept
      that it becomes Overdue tomorrow.
- [ ] Any B2B order placed today: check the warnings on the order detail
      page. Cut-off, dispatch day and credit limit warnings are the three
      that need a decision, not just a nod.
- [ ] Glance at **Orders** — anything in INQUIRY that should be CONFIRMED
      or cancelled.

---

## Weekly (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Products → economics**: is any product's margin drifting down
      because ingredient costs went up? Raise the price or fix the recipe.
- [ ] **Inventory**: any ingredient with a negative on-hand? That means a
      consumption was recorded without a matching purchase. Correct it
      with an ADJUSTMENT movement and a note.
- [ ] **B2B partners**: anyone over their credit limit for more than a
      week? That is a conversation, not a spreadsheet.

---

## Monthly (30 minutes)

- [ ] **Reports → generate** last month's report. Keep the PDF.
- [ ] **Export** the month's orders and payments CSV for your accountant.
- [ ] **B2B statements**: for every partner with a balance, download the
      statement CSV and send it. This is the entire credit-control process.
- [ ] **B2B margin by product**: any product now losing money at the
      contract price? Renegotiate at the next contract, not mid-term.
- [ ] **Contracts**: any expiring this month? Create the new version
      *before* the old one lapses, or orders silently fall back to retail
      prices.
- [ ] **Backup**: confirm the nightly backup file exists and is recent.

---

## Yearly

- [ ] Review every partner's credit limit against how they actually paid.
- [ ] Review minimum order values against your own costs.
- [ ] Re-price products whose ingredient costs have moved more than ~10%.
- [ ] New contract versions for the new financial year.
- [ ] Read the audit log for the year — it is the record of who changed
      what, and it is the first place to look when a number surprises you.

---

## The three habits that matter most

1. **IN PROGRESS when you start baking.** Everything about stock, cost and
   capacity depends on it.
2. **Payments as they arrive, with a reference.** A payment recorded a
   week later is a payment you cannot reconcile.
3. **Never edit a contract that has orders.** Close it, open a new one.
   Ten seconds now, or an unexplainable report later.
